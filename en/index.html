FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can't find your question? Feel free to get in touch. Even if you're not sure whether Nudge is for you, we're happy to think it through with you.

What does it cost?

Nudge is currently in its test phase, so taking part is free for now. We're testing how it works and what we offer, and based on the first experiences we'll decide how best to offer Nudge in the long run.

Do I need to have a company already?

No. You don't need to be registered with the KBO (the Belgian company register) and you don't need a business plan. Nudge is for anyone who runs a business or wants to start one: from someone with just an idea to a local self-employed professional who has been at it for years. Have a question or just want to bounce some ideas around? You're welcome.

What happens after I sign up?

After you sign up, we look at your question and search for someone who's a good fit. Once we've found a suitable match, we align the details and put you in touch.

How is my match chosen?

We mainly look at your concrete challenge and at the experience someone has with a similar challenge. That way we find someone who, from their own experience, can really make a difference to what you're dealing with today.

How quickly will I be matched?

That depends on your question and on who's available in our network. Sometimes we find a good match quickly, sometimes it takes a little longer. Either way, we'll keep you posted.

What happens during the conversation?

You decide together how long the conversation lasts and what you discuss. The content depends on your concrete question: maybe you want feedback on your idea, advice on a particular challenge or just to bounce ideas off someone with experience.

Nudge mainly gets the right person to the table. What you get out of the conversation is up to you.

I'm an experienced entrepreneur myself. Can I help?

Absolutely. Sign up as an experienced entrepreneur and we'll get in touch to see how you can help starters.

Am I tied to anything?

No. Taking part is completely non-binding for both sides. You decide whether you want to have a conversation and whether you'd like to stay in touch afterwards. As an experienced entrepreneur, you also decide when and how much you want to help.

Ready for a nudge forward?

Signing up only takes a few minutes. Tell us what's on your mind and we'll find someone who can help you from experience.
