FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Question not listed? Sign up and we’ll talk it through during the introduction call.

What does it cost?

The price depends on what you’re looking for, and we discuss it during the introduction call. That way you always know where you stand up front, and you don’t pay anything before we’ve talked.

When and how do I pay?

After the introduction call. You’ll receive a payment link by email so you can pay online.

What happens during the introduction call?

We get to know you and your business: your idea, the stage you’re at and the kind of person you’re looking for. That lets us search for the right match.

How quickly will I be matched?

That depends on what you’re looking for. Sometimes it’s very quick, sometimes the right match takes a little longer. Either way, we keep you posted.

I’m an experienced entrepreneur. Can I join?

Absolutely. Sign up as an experienced entrepreneur and we’ll get in touch to see how you can help starters.

Ready to take the first step?

Signing up only takes a few minutes. We’ll then contact you personally.
